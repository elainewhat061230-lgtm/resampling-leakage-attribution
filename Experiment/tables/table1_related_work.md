**Table 1. Prior work on pre-split resampling. Three of the four prior studies report almost exclusively prevalence-insensitive metrics (see Section 1 for the exceptions) and evaluate the incorrect arm on a resampled test set; the fourth reports prevalence-sensitive metrics but quotes its pre-split figures from earlier studies instead of running both protocols. No prior design attributes the inflation along a single path from the correct to the as-published protocol.**
